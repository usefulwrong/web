USEFULWRONG / ABOUT

An independent place to think in public.

A treadmill becomes a coat rack. A thousand assistants produce reports no one has time to read. Each is a small story about a useful solution and what it quietly changes.

What is UsefulWrong?

UsefulWrong publishes essays, illustrated stories and questions about the systems that shape ordinary life: tools, work, games, knowledge, attention and habit. The starting point is often something small enough to overlook.

Sometimes a familiar explanation works until one detail stops fitting. That is where the work begins.

What will you find here?

Right now: two complete English works — What Happens When the Routine Breaks? and A Thousand Agents and One Little King. You can read both without an account. Notes and references stay with the works.

How the work is made

Texts are edited as authored works, not generated on a posting schedule. AI may assist with research, editing or illustration; it does not make a claim true. Sources, interpretations and fictional examples should remain distinguishable.

The interactive pieces are optional. A beautiful illustration should never be necessary to understand an argument.

Can readers change an argument?

A well-supported correction can change a later edition. You can identify an exact passage and offer evidence or a counterexample. Public conversation currently happens on Reddit; there is no separate UW social platform.

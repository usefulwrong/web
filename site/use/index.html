USEFULWRONG / READING & REUSE

Read freely. Disagree precisely.

These notes explain what the website does, where public discussion happens and what permissions are — and are not — being offered.

Access

Articles are available to read without registration, payment or an email address. This website is a static publication hosted on a third-party platform. Some pages contain optional client-side interactions; essential article text remains readable when scripts are disabled.

Sources, revisions and uncertainty

A citation supports a specific claim; it does not make the entire argument true. A story or conceptual model is not automatically evidence about how people behave. Significant corrections may be reflected in a revised edition; readers can submit a source-backed objection through the feedback workflow.

Copyright and reuse

You are welcome to link to any public page. No blanket open license is granted for original writing, commissioned or generated illustrations, website artwork or other materials on this site. If you want to reproduce or adapt substantial material beyond what applicable law permits, request permission first. The rights of external sources remain with their respective holders.

Feedback and public discussion

The feedback composer runs in your browser and prepares text locally. Clicking “Copy” or “Download” does not transmit it to UsefulWrong. If you choose to publish it on Reddit, that post is public and subject to Reddit's rules and privacy practices. We may use a relevant argument or correction in a later edition, with appropriate attribution where warranted.

Privacy and third parties

No analytics tracker, advertising pixel, registration service or newsletter form is intentionally installed in this release. GitHub Pages, as the hosting provider, may log requests (including IP addresses) for security and operation. If you follow links to Reddit or other external sites, their policies apply. The website does not operate a private database of submitted feedback.

Publication status

These are independent essays and creative works, not personalized medical, legal, investment or career advice. Experimental illustrations are not scientific measurements unless explicitly documented as such.

For contact options, see Contact.
